# Health Roadmap — stellar, not freakish

Baseline (2026-07-10): 92.7 kg, ~27% body fat, 67.6 kg lean mass, BMI 29.2,
height ~178 cm. Breath hold already 4+ min dry (3+ verified underwater).

## The north star (infinitely improvable milestone)

**Increase adaptive reserve, decrease automatic reactivity.**

- Body: top 10–20% for age in aerobic capacity + useful strength, waist < half
  of height, normal BP / glucose / lipids, pain-free movement.
- Mind: shorter gap between stimulus and choice, longer sustained attention,
  faster recovery from fear and anger.
- People: more present and dependable as capability grows. If you get
  technically impressive but irritable and unavailable at home — that's failure,
  not progress.

This is unbounded in every direction, which is what an insatiable person needs:
you can chase it forever and it only ever *adds* health.

## Timeline tiers (realistic, from current baseline)

1. **12 weeks:** 86–88 kg, waist −5 cm or more, 3 strength + 2 easy aerobic
   sessions most weeks, sleep 7–9 h average. Get the baseline data: 7-day home
   BP average, waist measured properly, lipid panel + Lp(a) once, HbA1c,
   fasting glucose, kidney/liver panel. Optional but great for a systems guy:
   DEXA + real VO₂ test — a repeatable reference line.
2. **6–9 months:** 81–84 kg, ~17–20% body fat, visibly different, sub-25:00 5K
   in reach, intervals added after aerobic base.
3. **12–18 months:** 79–83 kg at 13–17% body fat with lean mass ≥ today's,
   VO₂ max ≈ 50 mL/kg/min, the capacity checklist below. This is "stellar,
   health-wise." Years 2–5 are integration: making it survive parenthood,
   travel, illness, deadlines.

Loss rate: ~0.4–0.7 kg/week via a 400–600 kcal deficit, judged on the 3-week
trend, never a single day. 130–150 g protein/day, 30 g+ fibre, alcohol rare.
Intermittent fasting stays — it's the correct abstainer tool — but it's an
organizational rule, not an endurance contest.

## Capacity checklist (the "amazing shape" definition at ~80–82 kg)

- 8–12 strict pull-ups; 30–40 clean push-ups; deadlift 1.25–1.5× bodyweight;
  bodyweight-total farmer carry.
- Sub-25:00 5K (or erg equivalent); then chase VO₂ 45 → 50 → 55.
- Pain-free deep squat, comfortable overhead reach, 60 s single-leg balance.

## Trophy goals — ambition that pays health dividends

Ego-driven goals are fine. The filter is: **measurable, finite season, and the
training for it makes you healthier even if you never hit the number.**

1. **Breath: 6:00 dry static apnea.** Verdict on 10 minutes: that is elite
   competitive freediving territory — years of specialized training, real
   blackout risk, and past ~6–7 min the marginal training is no longer
   health-positive. From your 4+ min, 5:00 is reachable in a season of CO₂/O₂
   tables; 6:00 is the "incredible" tier. Renegotiate only after you hold 6:00.
   Hard rules: never alone, never in/near water without a trained buddy, never
   after hyperventilation, never while driving. Join a freediving club — it
   converts the practice into events, which is your fuel.
2. **Mind: first jhāna, teacher-verified.** This is the real version of "gamma
   wave mind control." The famous gamma-synchrony monks had 10,000+ hours;
   gamma amplitude is a lab curiosity, not a goal. Jhāna access is a concrete,
   trainable, verifiable skill on a 1–3 year horizon for someone with vipassanā
   retreats already done. Path: daily floor (3 breaths minimum, 20–40 min on
   good days), one competent teacher (not technique-hopping), one supported
   retreat per year, concentration-focused (e.g. Brasington/TWIM lineage).
   The practical milestone that matters even more: agitated → alert-and-calm
   in under 10 minutes, on demand, mid-conflict.
3. **Engine: VO₂ max 50, then 55.** 50 ≈ 80th percentile for men 30–39, 55.5 ≈
   90th. Measured on a real test, not a watch estimate. This one number
   correlates with all-cause mortality better than almost anything else you
   can train — peak ego-health alignment.

One trophy per season is the main quest. The other two idle at floor level.

## Weekly frame (6–8 h total, family-compatible)

1. 3 × full-body strength (booked slots — calendar, class, or partner).
2. 2 × 40–60 min easy aerobic + 1 interval session (only after 4–6 weeks of
   base); 8–12k steps daily as the floor.
3. 2–3 yoga/mobility sessions (short counts) + one deliberately easy day —
   recovery is not allowed to become another competition.

## Safety rails (non-negotiable)

- Breath work: no hyperventilation before holds, no water/bath/shower holds,
  no cold immersion alone.
- Sleep: near-BMI-30 male — if snoring heavily, waking with headaches, or wife
  notices breathing pauses → screen for sleep apnoea before optimizing anything
  else.
- Meditation/breathwork causing insomnia, panic, or dissociation → reduce
  intensity and get experienced support. Intensity is not the metric.
- Medical check before hard intervals / long retentions / cold exposure if any
  red flags (chest discomfort, fainting, arrhythmia, family history of early
  sudden cardiac death).
