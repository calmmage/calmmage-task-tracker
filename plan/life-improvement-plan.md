# Life Improvement Plan — one day at a time

For: Petr Lavrov, 32. Systems-lover, novelty-seeker, list-of-3 enjoyer.

## Diagnosis first, no bullshit

The standard advice (fix sleep, meditate daily, exercise consistently, eat in
moderation) has failed you repeatedly. That is data, not a character flaw.
Your track record says you run on an **interest-based nervous system**: you are
world-class when there is *novelty, challenge, urgency, or stakes* (olympiad
golds, 4-min breath hold, quit smoking on a bet, Top-50 Beat Saber, 5+ career
switches) and you stall on anything that is *routine, moderate, or open-ended*
(daily meditation, diets, sleep schedules).

Three consequences:

1. **You are an abstainer, not a moderator.** Binary rules work (intermittent
   fasting is already working for you); "eat a little less" never will.
2. **You are event-driven, not routine-driven.** You organize hardcore yoga
   intensives but can't do 10 minutes of daily yoga. So: convert habits into
   events, challenges, and bets.
3. **Doing less kills your motivation.** So the fix for stress is not fewer
   ambitions — it's fewer *simultaneous open loops*. Containerize, don't shrink.

## Three principles

1. **Always come back (never miss twice).** Stop measuring streaks — they set
   you up to fail on day 4 and quit on day 5. Measure *return rate*: how fast
   you restart after a miss. Missing once is noise; missing twice is a decision.
2. **Floors, not goals.** Every habit has a laughably small minimum that counts
   as done: 1 breath of meditation, 10 push-ups, phone out of bedroom. Ceilings
   are unlimited on good days. The floor exists so the habit survives bad days.
3. **Make it a game with stakes.** Finite (2–6 week) challenges, a score, a bet,
   a public commitment. You quit smoking in a month *on a spor* — that's the
   engine. Use it deliberately instead of waiting for it to happen.

## The Daily 3

One day at a time means every morning you pick exactly three things
(list length 3, as the prophecy foretold):

1. **Focus** — the ONE project task that makes today a win.
2. **Body** — one physical thing (floor counts: walk, 10 push-ups, keep the
   fasting window).
3. **People** — one deliberate moment with wife or daughter (not logistics —
   attention).

Everything else is bonus. A day with 3/3 is a won day, even if the house is on
fire otherwise.

## Playbook per area

### Habits
- Run habits as **seasons**: pick ONE habit to install per 4-week season, with
  a challenge format and a stake. Rotate deliberately instead of abandoning
  chaotically — rotation is a feature for you, not a bug.
- Track only two numbers: days-since-last-done (return rate) and floor-hits
  this week.

### Goals & focus
- **WIP limit = 3.** One main quest (the season's project), one maintenance
  track, one wildcard for novelty cravings. Everything else goes to a
  **parking lot** file — written down so your brain releases it, not deleted.
- New shiny idea? It goes to the parking lot and may *swap in* at the next
  weekly review — never mid-week. The rule isn't "no new projects", it's
  "new projects wait max 6 days". Insatiability preserved, chaos contained.

### Health
- **Sleep:** don't fix the schedule, fix one anchor — your daughter's morning
  is a fixed wake time you already can't negotiate with. Protect it from the
  other side with a single floor rule: screens off / bed by a hard hour on
  work nights. One rule, binary, that's it.
- **Food:** intermittent fasting is the right tool for an abstainer — keep it,
  and add binary rules only (e.g. "no snacks outside the window"), never
  moderation rules.
- **Movement:** one *booked* hard session per week (class, partner, paid —
  an event with a start time), plus the daily floor. You'll never out-discipline
  a calendar invite.
- **Meditation:** stop trying to be a daily meditator. You're a vipassana /
  intensive type — book the retreats and breath-work sessions as events, and
  keep a 3-breath floor for daily life. That's a valid practice, not a failure.

### AI, productivity, cool projects
- **Make the system the project.** This repo is the tracker — building your own
  drill/review tooling aligns the novelty engine with the boring maintenance
  work. When the system feels stale, upgrading the system is a legal wildcard
  project.
- **Demo Friday:** every week, one shipped/showable slice of the main quest —
  posted to your digital community. Public cadence = stakes = your fuel.
- Timebox project work; unfinished ≠ failed, it's parked at a checkpoint note
  so re-entry is cheap.

### Love, family, wife & daughter
- Rituals beat minutes. Three standing ones:
  1. Daily 15 undistracted minutes with your daughter (phone in another room).
  2. Weekly wife date / walk — recurring slot, not "when free".
  3. Monthly philosophy evening at home — you already do this; it counts,
     take credit for it.
- Family is the third slot of the Daily 3 — it competes with nothing.

## The loop that holds it together

- **Morning (2 min):** pick the Daily 3. Say them out loud or write them here.
- **Evening (1 min):** score the day 0–3. No essays.
- **Weekly review (15 min, fixed slot):** return rates, parking-lot swaps,
  book next week's movement session, pick Demo Friday target.
- **Seasonal (every 4 weeks):** close the challenge, celebrate, pick the next
  season's habit + main quest + stake.

Missed the loop itself? Never miss twice. Come back tomorrow.
